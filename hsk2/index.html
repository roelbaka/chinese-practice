HSK标准教程2 · HSK Standard Course 2 (Lessons 1–15)

第一课 · 九月去北京旅游最好

September is the best time to visit Beijing · 要 (want to), 最 (most), approximate numbers with 几 and 多

Lesson 1

第二课 · 我每天六点起床

I get up at six every day · 是不是 questions, 每 (every), 多 + adjective (how tall / old)

Lesson 2

第三课 · 左边那个红色的是我的

The red one on the left is mine · 的-phrases (mine, yesterday's), verb + 一下, 真 + adjective

Lesson 3

第四课 · 这个工作是他帮我介绍的

He recommended me for this job · 是……的 (who did it), ……的时候 (when), 已经 (already)

Lesson 4

第五课 · 就买这件吧

Take this one · 就 (then / just), 还 + adj (passable), 有点儿 (a bit)

Lesson 5

第六课 · 你怎么不吃了

Why don't you eat more · 怎么 (how come), reduplicated measure words (天天, 个个), 因为……所以……

Lesson 6

第七课 · 你家离公司远吗

Do you live far from your company · 还 (still), 就 (as early as / only), 离 (distance), 呢 for emphasis

Lesson 7

第八课 · 让我想想再告诉你

Let me think about it and I'll tell you later · ……好吗？, 再 (again / then), pivot sentences with 让 / 请 / 叫, verb reduplication

Lesson 8

第九课 · 题太多，我没做完

There were too many questions; I didn't finish all of them · Result complements (看见, 听懂, 做完, 找到), 从……到……, 第一

Lesson 9

第十课 · 别找了，手机在桌子上呢

Stop looking for your cell phone; it's on the desk · 不要……了 / 别……了 (stop doing), 对 (good for, helpful to), 正在

Lesson 10

第十一课 · 他比我大三岁

He is three years older than me · Verb phrase + 的, 比 comparisons (and 没有), 可能 (maybe)

Lesson 11

第十二课 · 你穿得太少了

You wear too little · Degree complements (V + 得 + adj), 比 with 得

Lesson 12

第十三课 · 门开着呢

The door is open · 着 (ongoing state), 不是……吗 (rhetorical), 往 (towards), giving directions

Lesson 13

第十四课 · 你看过那个电影吗

Have you seen that movie · 过 (experience), 虽然……但是……, 次 (number of times)

Lesson 14

第十五课 · 新年就要到了

The New Year is coming · 快要 / 就要……了 (about to), 都……了 (already)
